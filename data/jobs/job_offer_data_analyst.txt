Data Analyst
Join our analytics team to:
- Develop and maintain ETL pipelines.
- Design and build interactive dashboards.
- Analyze large datasets and present insights to stakeholders.

Requirements:
- 2–4 years of experience as a data analyst or similar role.
- Proficiency in SQL and Python (pandas, NumPy).
- Experience with BI tools (Tableau, Power BI).
- Bachelor’s degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, or related field.

Preferred:
- Familiarity with cloud data platforms (BigQuery, Redshift).
- Knowledge of machine-learning basics.
